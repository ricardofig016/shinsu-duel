# How to Play Shinsu Duel

This guide teaches the basics. See the [rulebook](/rules?view=full) for the complete, authoritative set of rules.

Shinsu Duel is a 1vs1 card game set in SIU's Tower of God. If you have played other card games, the flow feels familiar: play cards from your hand, use your units' abilities, and destroy your opponent's base before they destroy yours. What makes this game its own are shinsu, lighthouses, and combat slots.

## The goal

You start with **20 lighthouses**. So does your opponent. Reduce them to **0 lighthouses** and you win.

Damage reaches the enemy lighthouses through cards and abilities.

## The board

Each player has a mirror of the same zones:

- **Deck and hand**: your 30-card deck on the left, the cards you hold on the right.
- **Lighthouses**: your life total, on the left.
- **Shinsu**: your energy for the round, on the right.
- **Battlefield**, in the center: two lines per player.
  - The **frontline** holds Fishermen, Scouts, and Wave Controllers.
  - The **backline** holds Spear Bearers, Light Bearers, Landmarks, and the Conduit.

The lines protect each other. Units can only target the enemy backline while the enemy frontline is empty, and can only target lighthouses while the whole enemy board is empty. Zone by zone, the details are in [Board](/rules?view=full#board).

## Shinsu, the economy

Shinsu pays for everything: playing cards and using most abilities. It lives in two pools.

1. At round start, your **normal pool** fills with shinsu equal to the **round number** (round 1 gives 1, round 5 gives 5, capped at 10 from round 10 on). The normal pool can never hold more than the round number.
2. At round end, up to **2 unspent shinsu** move to your **recharged pool**. Everything past that is lost.
3. Recharged shinsu, at most 2, is spent before the normal pool.

So the game rewards spending. Floating more than 2 shinsu at round end throws the rest away. The exact rules are in [Shinsu](/rules?view=full#shinsu).

## Combat slots

Both players have one **combat slot** per position, and the slot gates abilities.

- Using an ability spends the combat slot of the position the unit currently occupies, until the end of the round.
- One slot per position means one ability per position per round, no matter how many units stand there. Slots belong to positions, not units.
- Switching a unit's position spends the slot of the position it leaves.

Planning a round is mostly planning slots. Decide early which position attacks and which one supports. The full rules are in [Combat Slots](/rules?view=full#combat-slots).

## A round, step by step

1. **Round start**: combat slots reset, each player gains shinsu equal to the round number, and each player draws a card.
2. **Player turns**: you and your opponent alternate single actions. On your turn you do exactly one of the following, which end your turn.
   - Play a card.
   - Use an ability.
   - Switch a unit's position.
   - Pass.
3. **Round end**: when both players pass in a row, the round ends. Shinsu resets, saving at most 2 into the recharged pool.

Turns alternate without resetting between rounds. Whoever passes second hands the first action of the next round to their opponent, so timing your pass is part of the game. The full sequence is in [Gameplay](/rules?view=full#gameplay).

## Reading a card

- **Shinsu cost**: what you pay to play it.
- **Positions**: where the unit can stand. A unit with several positions picks one when deployed.
- **HP**: how much damage the unit takes before it dies.
- **Rank**: Regular, Ranker, or High Ranker. Dictates the rough shinsu cost of the unit.
- **Traits**: passive positive modifiers listed on the card.
- **Abilities**: actions that take your turn to use.
- **Passives**: abilities that are always on or trigger automatically.

Units, skills, and equipment behave differently. The differences are in [Cards](/rules?view=full#cards).

## Your first game

- Build a **30-card deck** with at most **3 copies** of each card.
- Draw **5 cards** at the start.
- Spend your shinsu. Anything past 2 at round end is gone.
- Think in slots. Before you spend an ability, check what other abilities you are giving up this round.
- Keep your frontline alive. While it stands, the enemy can't touch your backline or lighthouses.
